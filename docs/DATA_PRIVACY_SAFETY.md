# Data Privacy & Safety Policy — Sakhi Cycle

## 1. Privacy-First Principles
- **Local Storage by Default**: Menstrual dates, mood logs, and symptom histories are stored locally on the user's browser device.
- **No Third-Party Health Analytics**: Sensitive health metrics are never transmitted to third-party ad networks or tracking scripts.
- **Export & Import Data**: Users can download a JSON backup of their health data or delete it at any time.

---

## 2. Responsible Health UX & AI Safety
- **Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer**: Sakhi AI is an educational wellness tool and explicitly informs users that it is NOT a doctor.
- **Severe Symptom Guidance**: For severe or sudden symptoms (e.g. sharp unilateral pelvic pain, fainting), Sakhi AI directs users toward immediate medical evaluation.

---

## 3. Community Safety & Anonymity
- **Pseudonymous Identities**: Community forum posts and cycle buddy chats operate under pseudonyms (*Gentle Moon*, *Calm Bloom*) to protect identity.
- **Content Reporting & Moderation**: Users can flag inappropriate posts.
